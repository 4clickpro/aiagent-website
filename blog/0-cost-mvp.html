The Tech Stack: $0 to Launch

May 26, 2026

Building a functional Minimum Viable Product (MVP) doesn't have to burn a hole in your wallet. By leveraging generous free tiers and powerful open-source tools, you can get your app off the ground for exactly $0.

Here is the ultimate zero-cost tech stack for founders building intelligent apps and AI agents in 2026:

1. Frontend & Backend: Supabase

Ditch the expensive backend servers. Supabase is a free, open-source Firebase alternative that gives you a Postgres database, secure authentication, instant APIs, and real-time subscriptions right out of the box.

It integrates flawlessly with AI workflows, allowing you to store vector embeddings for RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) setups without paying a premium.

2. No-Code / UI: FlutterFlow

If you prefer building visually rather than coding every single line, FlutterFlow is the answer. It offers a generous free tier that lets you drag-and-drop your way to beautiful, responsive mobile and web apps.

You can connect it directly to Supabase with native integrations, meaning your UI and backend are talking to each other securely in minutes.

3. Hosting: Vercel or Netlify

When it's time to go live, deploy your frontend for free. Both Vercel and Netlify offer enterprise-grade global CDNs and seamless GitHub integration on their free tiers. Just push your code, and you're live to the world.

The takeaway: Launch smart. Scale when you need to. You have all the tools to build and test your ideas without upfront capital.
