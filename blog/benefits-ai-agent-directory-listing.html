Why Listing Your AI Agent in a Directory Can Help Customers Find You

November 3, 2026

A clear directory listing gives potential buyers a place to understand what your agent does, who it serves, and how to evaluate it.

Where to start

Write a specific description, identify the target customer, show supported integrations, and link to a working product page. Keep claims accurate and disclose sponsored placement clearly. A listing may improve discovery, but search ranking and sales are not guaranteed.
